# Which gift guide

Three formats. They are not three qualities of the same thing — they suit different
catalogues, different amounts of time, and different ambitions for the page.

| | **Full guide** | **Collection guide** | **Blog guide** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | A page template: banner, quick links, a gift group per audience, last-minute options, FAQ, SEO text | The store's own collection page, with a banner and quick links above it and SEO text below | An article on the store's blog |
| Built from | 7 sections + a page template | 3 sections wrapped around the store's own collection template | No theme files at all |
| Needs | Shopify CLI + a code editor | Shopify CLI + a code editor | Nothing. Runs from chat |
| Time | An hour or two | Twenty minutes | Twenty minutes |
| Ranks | Best. Most text, most structure, most internal links | Well, and it sorts and filters itself | Fine, and it is the easiest to get cited by an answer engine |

## How to choose

**The full guide** is right when the store sells to more than one kind of recipient, or
across more than one price band. Its whole advantage is that it answers several shoppers
on one page — the person who knows who they are buying for, the person who only knows
their budget, and the person who has left it too late. A store with eight products does
not need three gift groups and will look thin trying.

**The collection guide** is right when there is really one group of gifts, when the member
wants it live today, or when the catalogue moves enough that a hand-picked grid would go
stale. It inherits the theme's own sorting, filtering and pagination for free, which the
full guide does not have. It is also the honest choice for a store with a small range: a
single well-written collection beats a page padded out to look bigger.

**The blog guide** is right when there is no CLI, when the member wants something to link
from social and email this week, or when the goal is being *cited* rather than being
shopped — "the best coffee gifts under $50" is a question people ask an assistant, and a
well-structured article answers it better than a grid does. It is also the only format
that works entirely from chat.

**They are not exclusive.** The strongest shape is a full guide with blog articles
pointing into it, each article answering one question and linking to the matching gift
group. Say so when the member has time for both, but build one properly first.

## What stays the same in all three

- **The cut-off date is asked for, confirmed with its weekday, and never guessed.**
- **The catalogue is filtered the same way** — no drafts, no noindex discount duplicates,
  no app placeholder products, nothing at zero stock.
- **See-all and read-more links name the thing** they lead to.
- **The SEO text at the foot** is a paragraph per gift group, naming real products.
- **Nothing is published.** The member publishes.

## Switching later

A member who starts with the blog guide and then wants the full one loses nothing — the
worksheet answers, the product picks and the SEO paragraphs all carry across, and the
article should then link to the page. Going the other way, a full guide can seed a
collection guide in minutes. Ask which they want, but say that the answer is not permanent.
