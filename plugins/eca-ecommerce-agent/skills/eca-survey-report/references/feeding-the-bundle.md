# Where the findings go

A survey report that gets read and admired has failed. Everything it produces is an input
to work that happens somewhere else, and most of that work has a skill that already knows
how to do it well.

**Name the skill, hand over the specific finding, and say what to ask for.** A pointer
like "use this for your ads" is not a handover.

**Never assert a skill is installed.** Members have different bundles, and a handover
pointing at something they do not have is a dead end. Say "if `eca-ad-copywriting` is
installed" and give the finding anyway, so the work is doable either way. Most of these
live in the Marketing Coworker; `eca-landing-pages` is in the Operations Coworker, and the
product page builder is a separate free download rather than part of a bundle.

## The map

| What the report produced | Goes to | What to hand over |
|---|---|---|
| Angles, each with hooks | `eca-ad-copywriting` | The winning motivation, its share, and the hooks. It writes headlines and primary text from an angle. |
| Angles that suit video | `eca-ugc-scripts` | The motivation and the customer's own words. A real phrase from a buyer is a better hook than an invented one. |
| Objection counters, website | `eca-landing-pages` (Operations Coworker), or the free ECA Product Page download | The objection, its share, and what the follow-ups said made it matter. That is what a PDP section or a landing page block has to answer. |
| Objection counters, content | `eca-blog-writer` | The objection as a question a customer would type. Objections and search queries are usually the same sentence. |
| Objection counters, email | `eca-email-marketing-campaign` | The objection and the counter, for the pre-purchase flow. |
| Retention moves, repurchase triggers | `eca-email-marketing-campaign` | What made people come back, in their words, for the post-purchase and win-back flows. |
| Personas | `eca-persona-builder` | Hand them over as a cross-check, not a replacement. See below. |
| Alternatives considered | `eca-competitor-analysis` | The solution types people weighed up. Those are the real competitors, which are often not the ones the brand watches. |
| How they heard about us | `eca-meta-ads-4pi` | Self-reported attribution beside platform-reported. Where they disagree is worth knowing before budget moves. |

Only hand over what the data actually supports. An objection with four answers is not a
brief for a landing page, and the signal tags already say which is which.

## Personas: cross-check, do not overwrite

`eca-persona-builder` builds personas from reviews. This report builds them from survey
answers. They are different instruments and the difference is informative:

- **Reviews** are written after the product arrived, by people pleased enough to write.
- **Survey answers** come at the moment of purchase, from a broader and less self-selected
  group, including people who nearly did not buy.

Where they agree, confidence is high. **Where they disagree, say so rather than
reconciling it quietly.** A persona that reviews say is the core customer but surveys say
is a minority is a real finding about who writes reviews, not an error to be smoothed out.

## Write the findings back into the brand profile

This is the most valuable handover and the easiest to skip.

`eca-brand-intelligence` keeps `brand-data.md`, and much of it is inferred from reviews,
the website and market research. **Survey answers are primary research from actual buyers,
which outranks all of that.** Offer to update it:

| brand-data.md | What the survey settles |
|---|---|
| §2 Why people buy | the real ranked motivations, with shares |
| §5 Messaging pillars | which angle leads, by volume rather than by instinct |
| §9 Objections | the real ranked objections and why each one mattered |
| §11 Competitors | what buyers actually weighed up |
| §12 ICP & personas | who is really buying, and who they buy for |
| §17 Channels | self-reported attribution against assumed channel mix |
| §18 Sources & confidence | raise anything the survey confirms; flag anything it contradicts |

**Never rewrite the profile silently.** Show what would change, say how many answers sit
behind each change, and let the member accept it. A brand profile that quietly shifts
under someone is worse than one that is out of date, because they stop trusting it.

**Where the survey contradicts the profile, that is the headline.** A brand that believed
it sold on convenience and finds buyers choosing it on price has learned the most valuable
thing in the report, and it belongs in the three lines delivered in chat, not buried in a
section.
