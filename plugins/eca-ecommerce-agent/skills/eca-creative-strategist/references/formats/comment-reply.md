# Comment reply

A video that answers one real comment, question or objection shown on screen. It feels native, like you're just replying, not advertising.

**Funnel:** TOF, MOF and BOF. **Length:** 15 to 30 seconds. **Who:** the founder (best), the member, staff, a creator.

## Why it works

The comment on screen is the hook: it borrows curiosity and social proof, and frames the video as a helpful reply, not a pitch. Answering a doubt out loud disarms it for everyone watching who had the same doubt.

## Pick it when

- One objection blocks sales (from 1 to 3 star reviews, survey "what nearly stopped you", ad comments, DMs).
- The account read shows warm traffic that watches and doesn't buy.

## Sourcing the comment

1. **A real comment first.** Ask the member for one, or find it in their ad comments or reviews. Real always beats invented.
2. **If there isn't one**, offer 2 or 3 candidates built from the biggest purchase-blocking objections in the evidence, and let the member pick.
3. **Representative comments must stay honest.** Frame them as "a question we get a lot" or "someone always asks". Never attribute a made-up quote to a named person, and never fake a screenshot of a real account.

## Beats

| Beat | ~Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The comment | 3s | Comment on screen as a sticker; read it aloud with a reaction ("Okay, fair question.") |
| Validate it | 4s | Agree it's a real concern. Don't get defensive. "Honestly, I thought the same." |
| The answer | 8 to 12s | Specific, with proof. Demonstrate the thing the comment doubted |
| The turn | 4s | Why the doubt is exactly why the product exists, or why it's better than they feared |
| The ask | 3s | "So if you were wondering that too, [Brand], [offer], [site]." |

**One objection, done well.** One quick bonus point is fine only if it backs up the same objection. Don't pivot to a second objection; it splits the video.

## Shoot notes

Casual, reactive energy. A real comment screenshot (with permission and no identifying details) or a clean text sticker. The demonstration in the answer beat is the money shot.

## Sketch

Comment on screen: "Looks like it'd [doubt] tbh." → "Fair, I assumed that too." → (demonstrates) "It's actually [mechanism], so [result]. Watch." → "That's the whole reason we made it." → "If that was your worry, [Brand], [offer]."
