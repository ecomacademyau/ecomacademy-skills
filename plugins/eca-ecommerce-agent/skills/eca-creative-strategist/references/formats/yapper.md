# Yapper

One person, one take, talking straight to their phone like they're sending a friend a voice note with the camera on. Raw, a bit messy, and the workhorse format in most ECA member accounts right now.

**Funnel:** TOF (problem-led), MOF (objection-led). **Length:** 30 to 60 seconds. **Who:** a paid UGC creator who fits the customer, a real customer, the founder, the member.

## Why it works

It doesn't look like an ad, so people don't scroll past it like one. Eye contact plus a specific, lived problem gives the viewer the "that's me" moment in the first 3 seconds, and the one-take feel makes everything after it believable. Over-editing kills it: cuts, music and text-heavy edits turn it back into an ad.

## Pick it when

- There's a felt problem customers describe in their own words (reviews, survey).
- The person filming can talk about it honestly (they use it, or they're the founder).
- You're feeding the top, or iterating a winning angle with new openers.

Skip it when the whole value is visual and needs a demo to land (use problem-solution demo), or there's nobody who can talk about the product truthfully.

## Beats (on the spine)

| Beat | ~Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3s | Mid-thought, from `hooks.md`. Already moving or doing something in frame one |
| Problem | 3 to 10s | The lived version. One specific detail that proves it was real |
| Agitate | 10 to 18s | The workaround, what it cost, what they tried that didn't work |
| Solution | 18 to 22s | "Then I found these." Product up to the phone, front on, 2 seconds. No brand name |
| What makes it different | 22 to 32s | The mechanism, in plain words, while showing it |
| Proof | 32 to 42s | One or two things that convince: shown on camera, or a real review theme ("everyone in the reviews says...") |
| Result | 42 to 50s | What's different now; the feeling, plus one concrete detail |
| Offer and CTA | 50 to 58s | One casual line with the brand, the offer and the site. The real guarantee if there is one |

## Variants

- **Problem-led yapper** (TOF): the default above.
- **Objection-led yapper** (MOF): opens on the doubt ("I didn't think a $79 harness could be worth it"), then proof, then the turn.
- **Rant yapper** (TOF): opens annoyed about the category ("Why does every [category] [failure]?"), product as the relief. Never names or mocks a competitor.
- **Founder yapper**: the founder talking about one problem they solved, not the whole origin story (that's `founder-story.md`).

## Shoot notes

- One take. No cuts, or invisible ones only. Stumbles stay in.
- They move: change spot, grab the product, call someone in, all in the same shot. Movement is the re-hook.
- Product inside the take: front to the phone for 2 seconds, two detail close-ups, using or touching it.
- Natural light, phone at eye level, front camera. No ring light, no filters, no beauty mode, no music under the voice.
- Shoot the body once per hook, in one sitting: 3 to 5 takes.
- Captions added in the edit (eca-reel-editor), not burned in by the creator.

## Pitfalls

- A scripted read. If they're looking at a screen, it shows. Give talking points.
- Saying the brand early. Brand once, at the end.
- A list of features in the middle. One mechanism, shown.
- Ending on "link in bio" with no offer and no reason to tap now.
