# Unboxing / arrival

The package arriving, opened, and the first real reaction. Proves the product is real and shows what you actually get.

**Funnel:** MOF and BOF; TOF for gifting moments. **Length:** 15 to 30 seconds. **Who:** a creator, a customer, the member.

## Why it works

It answers "what do I actually get?" and "is it as good as the photos?" at once, and the unboxing itself is satisfying to watch. For gifts, it lets the viewer imagine someone's reaction.

## Pick it when

- The packaging, presentation or what's in the box is part of the value (gifts, kits, bundles, premium products).
- An objection is "is it worth the price?" or "is it real?".
- There's a gifting moment coming (Mother's Day, Christmas) and the angle is the reaction.

## Beats

| Beat | ~Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3s | The box in hand, already opening, with a line on who it's for or why it matters |
| Opening | 3 to 10s | Unhurried. Sound of the packaging (ASMR works). What's inside, piece by piece |
| First touch | 10 to 18s | Hold it, detail close-ups, the honest first reaction |
| One use | 18 to 24s | Trying it, briefly |
| Offer and CTA | 24 to 28s | Brand, offer, site |

## Rules

- No delivery promises ("arrived in two days") unless the brand guarantees it and the member approved the wording.
- A gifted unboxing is disclosed.
- Real first reaction, not a performed one.

## Shoot notes

Overhead or eye-level, natural light, a clean real surface. Hands in frame. Keep the sound; mute music. For ASMR, a quiet room and the phone close to the box.
