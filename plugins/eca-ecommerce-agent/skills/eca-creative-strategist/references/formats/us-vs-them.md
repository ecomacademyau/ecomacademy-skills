# Us vs them / old way vs new way

The old way (the generic version, the workaround, the category default) side by side with ours. Never a named competitor.

**Funnel:** TOF (conquests people who already use the alternative) and MOF. **Length:** 20 to 40 seconds. **Who:** a creator, the member, a customer.

## Why it works

The viewer already owns or uses the old way, so they recognise the problem instantly. Seeing both together does the comparing for them. It also gives Meta a structurally different ad from the usual talking head.

## Pick it when

- The real category (what customers buy instead) has a visible weakness that ours fixes.
- Reviews or survey answers mention what they switched from ("way better than the cheap ones").

Skip it when the difference can't be seen, or the honest comparison is close.

## Rules

- **Never name, show the logo of, or mock a competitor.** "A regular mask", "the cheap one", "what I used to use". (Comparative advertising that names brands carries legal risk; generic is safer and works as well.)
- The old way gets a fair showing. If it looks rigged, viewers trust nothing after it.
- Every difference claimed is shown or sourced.

## Beats

| Beat | ~Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3s | Both side by side in frame one, or the old one failing. "Regular [category] vs this." |
| The old way | 3 to 10s | Its specific failure, shown |
| Ours | 10 to 22s | The same test with ours. The mechanism named in plain words |
| One more difference | 22 to 30s | A second axis (price per use, comfort, how long it lasts), shown or sourced |
| Offer and CTA | 30 to 36s | Brand, offer, site |

## Variants

- **Split screen** (in the edit): same action, both products.
- **Hand swap**: one in each hand, test, bin the old one.
- **"What I used to do vs now"**: the old routine, then the new one, same person.

## Shoot notes

Same light, angle and conditions for both. One take per side. A pattern interrupt in frame one (binning the old one, the side-by-side reveal).
