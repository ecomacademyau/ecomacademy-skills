# 3 reasons why

A fast, punchy video that makes the value obvious by stacking exactly three sharp, specific reasons.

**Funnel:** TOF and MOF (people weighing it up). **Length:** 20 to 30 seconds (up to 45 only if a reason genuinely needs room). **Who:** anyone who uses it.

## Why it works

The viewer can feel the shape: a promise, three quick pay-offs, a nudge. Each reason re-hooks attention, so retention stays high. It reads well with the sound off when each reason is on screen. The trick is that the reasons are specific outcomes with proof, not feature bullets.

## Pick it when

- The evidence shows three distinct reasons people buy (three different axes of value, each with review or survey support).
- The viewer is comparing and needs the case made quickly.

Skip it when one reason dominates (make that one a yapper or demo instead), or the account already runs several list-style ads.

## Choosing the reasons

You choose them, from the evidence: the top three reasons to buy from customer voice, ranked, on **three distinct axes** (if two cover the same angle, drop one for the next one down). If the member already has three in mind, use theirs, but check each is benefit-led, distinct and supported, and say so if one is weak.

Hard three. Never two, never four. Lead with the strongest; don't save it.

## Beats

| Beat | ~Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 3s | The number and the claim, or the exact person. "Three reasons I'll never go back to [old way]." |
| Reason 1 (strongest) | 5 to 7s | The biggest benefit as an outcome, plus one proof or specific, shown |
| Reason 2 | 5 to 7s | A different axis of value. One concrete detail |
| Reason 3 (the clincher) | 5 to 7s | The objection-killer or the surprising one (price per use, "and it actually...") |
| The ask | 4s | Who it's for and the single action, with the brand and offer |

Every reason goes on screen as text too.

## Shoot notes

Punchy energy, quicker than a yapper. Counting on fingers or holding the product for each reason. A visual change per reason (new angle on the product, new spot). Light cuts are fine here, but keep it real.

## Sketch

Hook: "Three reasons I stopped buying [category] at the shop." → R1: "It's [outcome], [proof]." → R2: "Takes [time], no [hassle]." → R3: "And it's [clincher]." → Ask: "If you [situation], [Brand] has [offer]. [site]."
