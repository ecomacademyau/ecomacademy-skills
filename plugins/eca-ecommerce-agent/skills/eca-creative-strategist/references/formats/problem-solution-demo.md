# Problem-solution demo

The problem shown, then the product solving it on camera, start to finish, in one take. The camera does the convincing.

**Funnel:** TOF when the problem is visual and instantly recognisable; MOF when it's proving a claim to people weighing it up. **Length:** 20 to 45 seconds. **Who:** a creator, the member, staff, a customer.

## Why it works

Seeing beats being told. A viewer who watches the stain lift, the dog stop pulling or the zip go up one-handed doesn't need to trust the person talking. It also answers "does it actually work?" before it's asked.

## Pick it when

- The mechanism passes the demonstrable test: the camera can prove it in one take, unfiltered.
- The problem has a look: a mess, a struggle, a failure people recognise in a second.

Skip it when the benefit is a feeling over weeks (taste, calm, confidence), or the honest demo would take too long to watch.

## Beats

| Beat | ~Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3s | The problem happening, or the result shown first. Visual-led; the spoken line names the situation |
| Problem shown | 3 to 8s | The old way failing, on camera. Real, not exaggerated |
| Solution | 8 to 12s | Product in hand, front on. No brand name |
| The demo | 12 to 30s | Using it, start to finish, in one take. Narrate what's happening and the one mechanism that makes it work |
| Proof | 30 to 36s | The close-up of the result. A second quick test if it adds something (a different surface, a tougher case) |
| Offer and CTA | 36 to 42s | One casual line with the brand, offer, site |

## Shoot notes

- One continuous take for the demo itself. Cutting during a demo makes people think it was faked.
- Same light and angle for before and after. No filters.
- Real hands, real environment (the actual kitchen, the actual walk), not a studio table.
- Close-ups of two details that carry the mechanism.
- Before/after only under the rules in `compliance.md`.

## Pitfalls

- Staging the problem so badly it looks fake ("ugh, so frustrating!" with no real struggle).
- Demoing three features. Demo the one that matters to this angle.
- Speeding up footage or hiding a cut in the demo. Never.

## Sketch

Hook: (frame one: the failure happening) "This is what [problem] looks like every [time]." → Problem: (the old way failing) → Solution: "So I tried these." → Demo: (start to finish) "Watch, it [mechanism]..." → Proof: (close-up) "That's it. [Result]." → CTA: "It's [Brand], [offer], [site]."
