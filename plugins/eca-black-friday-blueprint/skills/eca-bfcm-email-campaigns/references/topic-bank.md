# Topic bank

Source: the member's own "Campaign Ideas" reference. Use this in Stage 2 to auto-fill topic suggestions for each slot in the calendar — but always **reframe every topic to name the actual offer/product**, never leave a generic label like "Hero main offer" sitting in the calendar unchanged. "Hero main offer" becomes "Launch: 40% Off Everything — 3 Days Only" once the real offer is known.

## Hero

- Hero main offer
- Launch / Last Chance
- Reminder
- Countdown
- Sale extended

## Product

- Best sellers
- Trending
- Top picks
- Daily deal
- Bundles
- Gift with purchase (GWP)
- Featured category
- Collection highlight
- Shop by size
- Shop under $X
- Low stock
- New styles added to sale

## Education

- Best product for X person (a use-case or gift-recipient angle)
- How to use
- Objection handling
- Myth busting
- Product/market comparisons
- Our process
- Problem > solution
- What makes our products different

## Plain text

- Warehouse updates
- Founder thank you
- A note from the CEO/founder
- Order refunded competition
- Giveaway

## Using the bank

1. Match slots to topics based on where they sit in the sale window: Launch/Last Chance/Countdown topics belong at the phase edges (see `content-ratios.md` on Hero placement); Best sellers/Trending/Bundles fill the Product-heavy middle; Education topics work well early (before the sale peaks, while there's room for a longer read) or as a change of pace mid-calendar; Plain text works well as a single, well-placed founder or brand-voice moment rather than clustered together.
2. Cross-check against Stage 3's data pull — if Shopify shows a genuine best-seller or Klaviyo shows a past email that performed strongly on one of these topics, prefer that real data over a generic pick from this list.
3. Don't force every topic in this file into the calendar — it's a menu, not a checklist. A short calendar might only use 4-5 of these; a long one might repeat a topic type (two different Product topics) but should never repeat the *same* topic twice in one calendar.
